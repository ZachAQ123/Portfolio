01About

I'm currently pursuing a Diploma in Mechatronics at Temasek Polytechnic, where I was awarded the Director's List Award and the Temasek Polytechnic Scholarship (Engineering) for placing among the top 10% of academic achievers.

My starting point was the Electronics and Microcontroller Club (EMC), which I joined in Year 1 with no experience in programming or microcontrollers. Since then I've taught myself Python, C++ for Arduino, and how ESP32 microcontrollers work — turning that into a growing list of hands-on hardware and software projects.

Outside the workshop, I served as Robotics Club CCA Chairperson and represented my school in national robotics and mathematics competitions, while staying involved in community projects like designing a colouring book for seniors and organising food donation drives.

02Projects
PRJ / 01FIRST BUILD
Go-Kart Speedometer

My first-ever project, built after joining EMC with zero prior experience. Measures go-kart wheel speed in real time using a Hall Effect sensor, housed in a custom 3D-printed mount. Required learning microcontroller programming, sensor calibration, and hardware troubleshooting from scratch.

Hall Effect SensorESP32C++3D Printing
PRJ / 02PROTOTYPE
IoT Recycling Bin

A team project built to remove the guesswork from waste sorting. Uses embedded sensors to detect and automatically sort waste, aimed at making sustainable habits effortless rather than relying on manual sorting.

IoTSensorsTeam Build

Built to fix a problem I experienced firsthand as a competitor — slow, error-prone manual scoring. A Flask-based server delivering live, reliable results, developed for RoboCoder Challenge, the competition I organise.
